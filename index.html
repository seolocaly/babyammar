His story

A tiny body, and a liver that is giving up

Ammar was born with biliary atresia. The ducts that should carry bile out of his liver are blocked, so the bile stays trapped inside and keeps damaging it.

The early operation that can sometimes help, called the Kasai procedure, was missed. Ammar now has chronic decompensated liver disease. His liver is failing.

His doctors at Dr. Rela Institute & Medical Centre in Chennai have advised a liver transplant at the earliest.

Every week matters for a baby this small. The family has done everything they can. They now need people like you.

A father's gift

Wasim is giving his son a part of himself

Ammar's father did not wait to be asked. He came forward on his own to donate a portion of his liver.

The doctors have declared both father and son fit for surgery. The medical team is ready to operate.

After the transplant, Ammar will need immunosuppressant medicines, steroids and calcium, and regular check-ups, so that his little body accepts his father's liver.

Wasim is giving everything he has. What he cannot give alone is ₹26,50,000.
